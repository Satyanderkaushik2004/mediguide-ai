About MediGuide AI

MediGuide AI is a general personal health assistant. It is not a medical provider and does not give medical diagnoses or prescriptions.

About MediGuide AI

MediGuide AI is an AI-powered health symptom assessment and medical document organization platform.

What it is

A symptom triage, document text extraction, and health timeline assistant designed to help you prepare for discussions with your healthcare provider.

What it is not

It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medications, or replace professional medical advice.

⚠️

This may require immediate medical attention

If you are experiencing severe chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, or another emergency, contact your local emergency service immediately.
